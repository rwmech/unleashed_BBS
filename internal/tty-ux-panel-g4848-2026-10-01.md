# The G4848 panel: a status layout for a 480 x 480 square

The Guition ESP32-S3-4848S040 (sold as AITRIP 4.0", G4848 1.0.0 on the
board-g4848 lane). Read against `release-prep/wt-g4848` at 26c6454:
`src/plugins/panel_gfx.h`, `src/plugins/panel.cpp`, `src/board.h`,
`src/platform/platform_esp32_st7701.cpp`, `SKINS.md`, and the earlier specs
(`internal/tty-ux-panel-2026-09-24.md`,
`release-prep/wt-mf35/internal/tty-ux-panel-mf35-2026-09-26.md`,
`internal/tty-ux-panel-ws43b-2026-09-28.md`,
`internal/tty-ux-panel-gallery-2026-09-28.md`). Every box below is computed
from the pixel grid and the Spleen cell sizes in `panel_gfx.h`; the mock-ups
are generated from those coordinates, not typed.

## The glass

- 480 x 480, ST7701S on the RGB bus, the driver's own 480 x 480 framebuffer
  in PSRAM streamed by DMA at 12 MHz. `BBS_LCD_SCALE` 1: one drawn pixel is
  one glass pixel.
- 4.0" diagonal is 71.8 mm a side, **0.150 mm a pixel**, 170 ppi. That is
  the Makerfabs 3.5" (0.153) to within 2%, so every size the MF35 spec
  settled holds here: 8 x 16 text is 1.2 x 2.4 mm, 16 x 32 is 2.4 x 4.8 mm,
  a 1 px rule is visible, strokes stay 2 px. Computed from the advertised
  diagonal, not measured.
- 60 glyph columns by 30 glyph rows at 8 x 16.
- A band is 480 x 24 = 11,520 px (`kBandPixels`), a 23 KB PSRAM copy, no
  wire. A whole-glass redraw is 20 bands. Today's 480 x 320 picture is 14.
- It is an "86 box" panel: sized for a wall-switch box. Expect it on a wall
  or a desk stand and read from a metre or two, not held.
- Touch: GT911 polled every 50 ms (`BBS_TOUCH_POLL`). Taps only; no
  position is read.

## The verdict

Fixable, and cheap. Today the board draws the Makerfabs' 480 x 320 layout
80 rows down (`board.h:1379-1383`, `BBS_RGB_YOFF 80`), so 153,600 of the
glass's 230,400 pixels, a third, are black bands, and the layout inside them
is still the crowded MF35 one: handles cut at 12 on a glass with room for
20, a right column 160 px wide carrying four jobs. The square fixes itself
by one reflow: the MF35's right column (calls, recent, traffic, cells) moves
under the node board into the 160 new rows, and the node board takes the
full 480. Every drawing function already exists. The new code is one
`bigLayout` branch of coordinates, a LEFT column, a light-bar foot the 4.3B
already has, and four small fixes that the MF35 needs too. No new font, no
new widget, about 300 bytes of static DRAM.

### 2. `bigLayout` has no square, so a 480 x 480 picture would be worse

`panel_gfx.h:1217` `bigGlass(480, 480)` is true and `:1229` sets
`land = w >= h`, so a 480 x 480 picture takes the landscape branch: the
same layout at the top, 160 black rows at the bottom. Changing the profile
without the layout moves the black band, it does not remove it. Both land
in one change.

### 3. Handles are cut at 12 on a glass that holds 20

`panel.cpp:1572` cuts the handle at `12 * kSmallW`; `BBS_USER_MAX` is 20.
`breadbin_commodore64` reads `breadbin_com`. The 36-glyph row was sized for
the MF35's left column, 296 px. The square gives the row 462 px.

### 4. A tap on the big glass holds the page 3 s, not 10

`panel.cpp:1886-1911` `touchTick` sets `g_nextLong` for a tapped page, and
the small layouts' `slotTick` honours it (`:718`, `:731`) and holds the
name while asleep (`:715`). `bigSlotTick` (`:1474-1515`) does neither: it
always holds `kHoldMs`. On the G4848, the first big glass with a polled
touch, a person taps to read the address into a phone and gets 3 s. The
4.3B spec settled 10 s for exactly this.

Rule: `bigSlotTick` takes the same three lines as `slotTick`: hold
`g_holdLong ? kTapHoldMs : kHoldMs`, move `g_nextLong` into `g_holdLong` when
a fade-in ends, and `g_stepAt = now` while `g_asleep`.

### 5. A caller's snap shows as a login, in the ringing colour

`panel.cpp:1664-1669` `bigRecent` knows login, guest, logoff and bell. An
`EV_SNAP` falls through to `kIconLogin` in `kBusy`, so a snap reads as an
orange login. The small layouts draw it as `kIconCamera` in `kDial`
(`:901-903`). The G4848 has no camera, but its sats file into Photos and
`photos::callerSnaps` feeds the event (`:1280-1290`).

Rule: `bigRecent` takes the small layout's two ternaries: `EV_SNAP` is
`kIconCamera`, colour `kDial`.

### 6. The heap wears the chip, and the chip's temperature is missing

`panel.cpp:1744` draws the heap with `kIconChip`. The 4.3B spec revision 1
settled that the chip is the chip's temperature and memory is `kIconRam`,
so the two never share a picture. The G4848 profile does not define
`BBS_HAS_CHIP_TEMP` although every ESP32-S3 has the sensor, and the
WROOM-1 N16R8 is rated to 65 C ambient (the WS2 note, `board.h:1037`). In
an 86 box behind a backlight, die temperature is a figure worth having.

Rule: the square's heap cell is `kIconRam`, its temperature cell is
`kIconChip`; the profile adds `BBS_HAS_CHIP_TEMP 1` and
`BBS_PANEL_TEMP_WARM 65`.

### 7. The LEDs are 220 px of beads on a 480 px foot, and the drive lamp is not shown

`panel.cpp:1792` draws the big glass's strip as `bigLedAt` squares, 10
cells of 22 from x 130, and `bigLayout` leaves `L.drive` empty. The G4848,
like the 4.3B, has no pixel wired (`board.h:1321-1324`, lights on with no
pin, `switchboard`). The 4.3B revision 1 answered this glass's problem: a
light bar across the foot, where the number of lit segments is the number
of callers from across a room, and the drive lamp the lights plugin already
computes, at the left.

### 8. Closed and shutting down say so in one 8 x 16 line only

`panel.cpp:1683-1694` `bigWord` puts `CLOSED to callers` or `SHUTTING DOWN`
in the band, and every free row still says `free` (`:1595`) while the
switchboard effect keeps the free lamps lit in dial blue, which means "a
caller can dial into this". On a closed board neither is true. The word
also has no countdown: `Bbs::shutEnds_` is private.

## What the 160 rows buy

The header's 43 rows and the node board's rows are at the same y as the
MF35's. The 160 rows hold the deck that sat beside the board on the MF35,
and that is what frees the full width for the board.

| | MF35 landscape, today on the G4848 | Square |
|---|---|---|
| glass drawn | 480 x 320 of 480 x 480, 67% | 480 x 480, 100% |
| node row | 288 px, 36 glyphs | 462 px, 57 glyphs |
| handle | 12 glyphs, cut | 20, whole (`BBS_USER_MAX`) |
| columns | node, handle, doing, on, term | the same plus LEFT, 24 px gutters |
| recent events | 4, handle cut at 11 | 6, handle whole |
| traffic sweep | 160 columns, 10.7 min | 228 columns, 15.2 min |
| system cells | 6 (one, the camera, never built) | 6, with the chip's temperature |
| strip | 10 squares, 220 px | a light bar, 426 px, and the drive lamp |
| a ring | 8 x 16 in the slot | 16 x 32 across the whole header |

Ideas weighed and not taken:

- **Node rows in 16 x 32.** Eleven rows at a 36 px pitch is 396 px, and 30
  glyphs a row cannot carry the handle, doing and terminal. Rejected.
- **A 24 px pitch for the node rows.** 44 px for 4 px more air a row costs
  two of the six recent events. The width is what makes the rows read here:
  whole handles and 24 px gutters. Pitch stays 20, every big glass's.
- **A recent-photo thumbnail strip.** Four 4:3 thumbnails across the deck
  are 112 x 84 px each, 16.8 x 12.6 mm: recognisable, not useful. The 1.2.1
  show already gives each new photo 456 x 342 px, 68 x 51 mm, for a minute.
  A strip would need a decode per new photo or a kept cache (4 x 18,816 B
  PSRAM), a second path under the "Photos on the panel" privacy switch, and
  a 100 px hole on every board with no camera or sat, the G4848 included.
  Revisit when the swipe gallery leaves the backlog: one tile as Photos'
  door, tapped to open it.
- **The caller's address in the row.** The glass faces the room. No.

## The header page-turn by touch

- A tap anywhere on a lit status panel turns the slot to its other page
  now, and that page holds 10 s (`kTapHoldMs`). Two pages, both ways to
  dial the board: `192.168.0.86:6400` and `rustyantenna.local:6400`. That is
  the only thing a tap does on the status panel. No regions: the 4.3B
  spec's rule, a tap does one thing a person can predict.
- A tap on a sleeping panel (Sleep min) wakes it and is spent.
- While a ring shows, a tap does nothing to the header.
- While the new-photo show is up (1.2.1), a tap ends it at once and the
  status panel comes back. With no gallery in 1.2.1 there is nothing else a
  tap could mean there.
- Silent: not polled, as now.
- It needs finding 4's fix to hold 10 s on this glass.
- Unverified on the glass: the lane's PANEL capture says `Touch quiet, 0
  taps` (`release-prep/g4848/panel.txt`), so the GT911 had not sent a report
  when it was taken. The controller has no reset line here and picks 0x5D
  or 0x14 at power-up. A tap turning the header is the check.

- **Closed** (`syscfg::get().closed`): the band word `CLOSED to callers` in
  `kWarm`, as shipped. Every free row says `closed` in `kWarm` at 2/3
  (`scale(kWarm, 2, 3)`) instead of `free`. Ten warm rows is a lot of warm,
  and that is the point: a fresh board starts closed, and the sysop setting
  it up should see from across the room why nobody can call. Row S keeps
  `sysop line`, because the first account can still come in on a closed
  board.
- **Shutting down** (`listening() && !answering()`): the band word
  `SHUTTING DOWN in 1:59` in `kRisk`, the count once a second (one 3,936 px
  box a second); when the count is done and the board still runs, `SHUT
  DOWN, restart the board` (28 glyphs of 30). Free rows say `not answering`
  in `kRisk` at 2/3. Callers' rows stay as they are until the board drops
  them.
- **The lamps follow**: switchboard's free lamps go dark, not dim blue,
  while the board is closed or not answering. Dim blue means "a caller can
  dial into this", which is false. Dark, not warm: amber is the drive
  lamp's idle glow beside them. The lights plugin's change, and right for a
  wired strip too.
- **Silent** (the switch or silent hours): backlight off, nothing drawn,
  touch not polled, as on every glass. When it ends, the whole glass is
  redrawn (20 bands) and the backlight is owed until it is whole; the
  driver fades it up over 300 ms (`platform_esp32_st7701.cpp:602-607`).
- **A ring**: the bar and band become one 42 px block in `kBar`, the words
  in 16 x 32 `kBusy` at y 5, centred, at most 29 glyphs (the handle cut at
  18 so ` is ringing` stays whole). The name, slot, glyph strip, band word,
  antenna, dBm and clock are not drawn while it shows, and the bell glyph's
  2 Hz blink stops: the banner is the bell. Steady, not blinking, because a
  20,160 px box at 2 Hz is four bands a second for nothing. When the ring
  ends, the bar and band are refilled and their fields invalidated: two
  bands. At 4.8 mm a glyph this is the one message on the panel readable
  from across a room, which is the 4.3B's finding 1 answered on the big
  glass. The track and the dot stay.

## Skins on the square

- A skin names the glass it was drawn for (`panel W H`). A square skin is
  `panel 480 480`, its own size: drawn whole, decoded into a 460,800 B
  background, LED weights capped at 131,072 B as now. During a change of
  skin both blocks are in PSRAM, about 1.2 MB of the 8 MB.
- **A 480 x 320 skin is offered on the square, framed.** Today it works on
  the G4848 by accident (the picture is 480 x 320, so `panel 480 320`
  matches). The moment the picture becomes 480 x 480 it would stop matching,
  and every stock skin, all of which are 480 x 320, would vanish from
  CONFIG. So the square layout and this rule ship together.
- Framed: drawn at y 80 with its own coordinates (the Scene adds 80 to
  every y), rows 0..78 and 401..479 in `kBg`, and a 1 px `kRule` keyline at
  y 79 and y 400. No status parts in the bands: a skin is a whole picture,
  and the header above it would be two systems on one glass. A picture in a
  mat reads as a picture; that is the same judgement as the photo show.
- PANEL says `Skin c64 (480 x 320, framed)`. CONFIG lists both sizes in
  name order, as now.
- Any other size is refused, as now.

## Generic or board-specific

Generic, chosen by the glass's size, not by the board: any 480 x 480 glass
takes this layout. The 86-box clones the site lists as also compatible run
this image already; a Waveshare 480 x 480 profiled later would get it for
nothing.

- `bigLayout`: a square branch first, `w == 480 && h == 480`, before the
  landscape test at `panel_gfx.h:1229`.
- A compile-time gate for the two constants that grow, so the MF35's
  objects stay what they are: `BBS_PANEL_SQUARE` in board.h's display block,
  `(BBS_LCD_WIDTH == 480 && BBS_LCD_HEIGHT == 480)`; `kRecent` is 6 with it
  and 4 without; `kGraphCols` 228 with it and 160 without (`g_gHead` is a
  `uint8_t`, fine below 256).
- Exactly 480, not "square": every box above is absolute and the deck's
  columns are sized to 480. A 720 x 720 glass is its own spec, and cannot
  borrow the 4.3B's whole-number doubling.
- An RGB panel is never turned (`panel.cpp:2028-2033`), and a square has no
  portrait to offer, so there is no second orientation to specify.

## What stays as it is

- The header, box for box: the square is 480 wide like the MF35, so the
  bar, band, glyph strip, band word, antenna, dBm, clock, track and dot draw
  through the shipped code at the same x. Only the ring banner is new up
  there.
- The two-page slot (both ways to dial), the permanent name. The uptime and
  card stay in the cells.
- The fixed eleven-row node board, its 20 px pitch, the rank colours, the
  pips, the words `free` and `sysop line`, hidden staff drawn as free.
- 8 x 16 text everywhere but the ring. At 0.150 mm a pixel it is the MF35's
  reading size, which was right.
- The sweep: fixed log scale, a column every 4 s, never scrolls.
- The cell figures and their colour thresholds.
- `switchboard` as this board's strip effect, and `drawLed`'s lit and off
  styles.
- The MF35's 480 x 320 and 320 x 480 layouts, untouched; every other
  glass's too.
- Silent: dark, nothing drawn, the light owed until a whole frame is out.
- `DirtyCheap`, and one band a tick.

## Implementation order

Cheapest and most visible first. Items 1 and 6 ship together in 1.2.1,
before the G4848 goes on the installer, or the stock skins disappear from
its CONFIG.

- **1. The glass full.** Profile: `BBS_LCD_HEIGHT 480`, `BBS_RGB_YOFF 0`,
  `BBS_LCD_RAM_SHORT 480`, `BBS_LCD_RAM_LONG 480`, `BBS_HAS_CHIP_TEMP 1`,
  `BBS_PANEL_TEMP_WARM 65`. `BBS_PANEL_SQUARE` and the two constants.
  `bigLayout`'s square branch with every box above, including `L.drive`,
  `L.leds` and `L.ledBar = true` so `refreshLeds` draws the light bar
  through `segAt` (`bigLedAt` only when not square). `BigLayout` gains the
  handle's width and the LEFT column's right edge beside `colDoing`,
  `colOnEnd` and `colTerm`. `bigRow` takes the handle width from the layout
  (20 glyphs here, 12 on the MF35) and draws LEFT; `bigHead` draws the LEFT
  label; the cells map to the table above. About 1 KB of flash.
- **2. Two fixes the MF35 shares**: `bigRecent`'s snap (finding 5) and the
  heap on `kIconRam` with the temperature cell (finding 6). These change
  the MF35's object, deliberately: they are its bugs too.
- **3. The tap's 10 s** in `bigSlotTick` (finding 4).
- **4. Closed and shutting down**: the row words, the band word's count,
  and switchboard's dark free lamps. Needs a public
  `uint32_t Bbs::shutdownLeftMs(uint32_t now) const` (0 when none); the
  lights plugin reads `Bbs::answering()` and `syscfg::get().closed` for
  the lamps.
- **5. The ring banner**: one field (`F_RING`, appended after the recent
  rows), one bool, the header's fields invalidated when it ends.
- **6. Skins**: `panel 480 480` native, `panel 480 320` framed (a Scene
  origin of two `int16_t`, the choice list taking both sizes, PANEL's
  word).
- **7. The photo show's square geometry**, built with 1.2.1's show.
- **8. Square stock skins**, screen-artist, when there is time.

What it costs, all on S3 profiles with `BBS_PANEL_SQUARE`:

- Static DRAM: `g_shown` two more rows, 160 B; the sweep's ring 2 x 68 more,
  136 B; the ring banner's bool. About 300 B of the G4848's 74,104 free.
- PSRAM: the panel's canvas 460,800 B, up 153,600 from 307,200, beside the
  driver's own 460,800 B. The photo buffer 342,792 B while a show is up. A
  square skin about 600 KB. All of it inside 8 MB with room left.
- Optional, later: the driver could scan out the panel's canvas directly
  and drop its own copy (460,800 B and the band copy), at the price of a
  tear on a box drawn mid-frame. Not needed at 8 MB.
- `host/test_panel.cpp` gains the square's boxes the way it holds the
  others': each inside 480 x 480, none overlapping, the vertical budget
  summing to 480, the foot under one band.
